## 2. Hosting on GitHub Pages

The site is served by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open a page, GitHub processes technically necessary data, in particular your IP address, the date and time of access, the requested address and browser and system details. These are kept in server logs to deliver the site securely and to defend against attacks.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate interest is delivering the website securely and reliably. Data may be transferred to the USA. GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, which is covered by an adequacy decision of the European Commission. More information: [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## 3. No cookies, no third-party content

This website sets no cookies. Fonts and scripts are loaded from this website itself; no third-party content such as Google Fonts or content delivery networks is embedded.

If you switch between light and dark mode, your browser stores that setting locally (local storage). It stays on your device and is not sent to me. Storing it is necessary for the function you asked for (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).
